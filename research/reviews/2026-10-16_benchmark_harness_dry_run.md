# Benchmark harness: exploratory dry run (2026-10-16) — a statement about the harness, not about multiscale features

Registration (before any benchmark number): `research/plans/2026-10-16_benchmark_harness_dry_run_registration.md`. Code `src/ams_ecal/benchmarks/learning_curves.py` (7 tests: balanced log-loss, metric counts against the calibration threshold, disjoint roles, tabular models on a signal and on a null, the network on synthetic images and its CPU reproducibility, paired differences). Results `results/benchmarks/harness_dry_run.json`. Electrons and ALL protons of the development-large sets at 20 GeV deposit, 100 GeV deposit and 100 GeV readout; 4 training sizes (100, 300, 1,000, 3,000 per class) x 5 repeats; 11 models (energy-only, `conv`, `conv_noE`, `conv_noE_nocells`, each with and without the 24 two-dimensional multiscale statistics for the last three, a standardized logistic regression on `conv`, and a small convolutional network on the raw 18 x 72 image, PyTorch on the RTX 4050); roles disjoint by construction and checked. PyTorch 2.14.1 + CUDA 13.0, installed 2026-10-16. No sealed set read.

## 1. Cost (sets the budget of the registered benchmarks)

Whole dry run: 313 s for three blocks. Boosted trees: 0.1 to 0.4 s per fit (100 to 3,000 events per class); logistic regression 0.02 to 0.03 s; the network 0.6 s at 100 per class and **6.3 s at 3,000 per class** (30 epochs), peak GPU memory 715 MB of 6,141 MB. At 10^6 events the network's cost grows with the number of events (about 6.3 s per 6,000 events here); the cost at 10^6 would have to be measured, not extrapolated; the memory is not the limit on this card for a model of this size.

## 2. Controls (registration section 4: same-class AUC must be 0.5 within 0.02)

Seven of the nine checks pass; two **fail the registered tolerance**: 20 GeV deposit, boosted trees 0.527 and logistic regression 0.537 (the other values 0.490 to 0.516; the network 0.492, 0.503, 0.494). Checked by repeating the null over 12 seeds on the 20 GeV deposit electrons: at 1,000 per class the mean AUC is 0.5042 (boosted trees) and 0.5085 (logistic), the standard deviation over seeds 0.008 (the theoretical standard deviation of an AUC at this size is 0.013); at 2,500 per class 0.5003 and 0.5019, no value outside 0.5 +- 0.02. **Reading:** the tolerance of 0.02 is only 1.5 standard errors at 1,000 per class, so a failure by chance is expected in about one check in ten and the registered control is badly calibrated for this size, not evidence of a leak. A small positive offset of the logistic null at 1,000 per class (+0.0085, 3.5 standard errors of the mean over seeds, but the 12 draws share one pool of 10,000 events, so the seeds are not independent) is **unexplained** and must be re-examined on independent pools before the registered protocol.

## 3. What the ideal regime shows (descriptive; not a result on the hypotheses)

- The problem is saturated: AUC 0.995 to 1.000 for every model and size, including the energy-only model (0.995 to 1.000), as in the S0 audit. Log-loss falls to 0.001 to 0.02 for every rung at 1,000 or more events per class, so rungs cannot be told apart there; only at 100 to 300 per class do the harder rungs (`conv_noE`, `conv_noE_nocells`) show headroom (log-loss 0.03 to 0.10).
- **The rejection is censored by the size of the evaluation set**: with 4,000 evaluation protons, zero protons passing a 90% electron-efficiency threshold gives a lower limit of 1,336, which is the value in 78% of all points (maximum 1,336). A registered benchmark needs far more protons (about 3 x 10^4 per claimed 10^4 rejection, about 10^6 to measure it), the roadmap's production size.
- **Paired difference of log-loss "rung with multiscale minus rung without"** (36 rung-size-block cells, spread over 5 repeats): mostly within the spread; the means are 0.000 to 0.003 for `conv` and up to +0.025 or -0.007 for `conv_noE` at 100 to 300 per class with a spread of 0.02 to 0.06; four of 36 cells have the mean more than 2 standard errors of the mean below zero (multiscale better) and one above. The dry run draws no inference (5 repeats; spread is not an interval); nothing follows about the multiscale features.
- The raw-image network has the **lowest log-loss at 100 per class** (0.012 to 0.024 against 0.027 to 0.049 for boosted trees on the conventional features at 100 GeV) and equals the others at 1,000 or more per class: an exploratory observation in an ideal regime with 5 repeats, not a claim.

## 4. Harness defects found, to fix before the registered protocol is written

1. Same-class null: size at least 2,500 per class and a tolerance stated in standard errors (3 standard errors of the AUC), with independent pools per seed.
2. Evaluation set: at least 3 x 10^4 evaluation protons (the realistic production) so that rejection is not capped by the evaluation size; report the censored lower limit as such.
3. The ideal regime cannot discriminate rungs at 1,000 or more events per class: the registered learning curves belong to the detector-level, realistic-regime dataset, with the P5 hard subset defined by observables and frozen in the amendment addendum.
4. At least 10 repeats and an uncertainty procedure that accounts for the correlation between folds (nested or whole-pipeline bootstrap), not the spread over seeds.
5. Re-check the unexplained logistic offset (section 2).
