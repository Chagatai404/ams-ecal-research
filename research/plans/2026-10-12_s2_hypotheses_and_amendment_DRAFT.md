## 2. Rules for interpretation (finite-scale language)

1. The default name of the quantity is a **finite-scale heterogeneity statistic**. The words **fractal**, **scaling**, **multifractal** are used only when **all** of (a) the estimator controls (DEC-013) pass for that window and representation; (b) the statistic differs from both surrogates (cell-shuffle and smooth-plus-Poisson, same hit count and layer energies) beyond their 95% bands; (c) for "multifractal", D0 >= D1 >= D2 with non-overlapping intervals for at least three orders, and the q-dependence exceeds the surrogates'. On the 18 x 72 readout (about 1.26 decades, six box sides) condition (c) is **not expected to be met**, so those words are not used for readout results.
2. Every statement names its scope: simulator, version, physics list, energy, representation (deposition / fibre-energy readout / voxels), eligibility. "Not shown on data" is part of the sentence.
3. Statements of novelty are written "not found in the searches of 2026-10-12 (scope in the verification record, section 4)", never "first".
4. An observed difference is attributed to **multiscale structure** only after it survives matching on occupied cells and total deposited energy; before that it is called a difference in occupancy and energy.
5. A negative or null result is reported with its power statement (the smallest planted structure the statistic can detect).

3. **Freezing rule (new, because S4 has not run).** The window, box sizes, strata and eligibility thresholds named "frozen by a dated addendum" are fixed in a **dated addendum written after S4 and before the first look at any confirmatory event**, from rules, not from the data: the widest window that passes the 3D estimator controls (DEC-013 criteria), box sizes in mm, strata by equal-count quintiles of occupied voxels and of total energy. After the addendum is committed (tag + hash) the primaries cannot be changed; a changed primary is a new registration and the set that was looked at is consumed.
4. **Planted positive control (P1).** Before P1 is run on confirmatory data, the statistic must **recover a planted loss of lateral organisation at fixed occupancy**: real electron events against copies with a fraction phi of the occupied cells permuted within each layer (phi = 1 is the full cell-shuffle). P1 is run only if the statistic detects phi = 1 with power >= 0.90 at the planned sample size and rejects at most 10% of the time for two independent halves of the same class; the smallest detected phi is reported as the power statement of any null.
5. **P2 decision rule.** A level is "survived" if KS_level exceeds the upper 95% limit of the cell-shuffle surrogate at that level and has the same sign as at the fine level. Levels with fewer than 100 eligible events in either class are **"not testable"** and are listed, not analysed. The reported result is the curve over the four levels.
6. **Not testable cells.** Cells (energy x representation x window) with too few electrons or protons eligible are declared not testable before analysis, using `missingness_controls.testability` (minimum 100 per class).
7. **Multiplicity.** Holm across the five primaries at alpha = 0.05; Benjamini-Hochberg q <= 0.05 inside a family for secondaries; secondaries are labelled **exploratory** unless their family's primary is confirmed (`multiplicity.decide`). Permutation tests use 10,000 draws (the smallest reportable p is 1e-4; Holm with five families needs p <= 0.01).
8. **Data roles.** New sealed sets per purpose with 63-bit seeds (DECIDE-2); the existing sealed sets stay closed; train / calibrate / evaluate roles disjoint (`rejection_limits.assert_disjoint_roles`); the P4 threshold from independent calibration electrons.
9. **What the amendment does not change.** The estimator definitions, the eligibility thresholds 336 / 572 (flagged weak; replaced by the 3D rule in the addendum), the ideal-regime development readings (exploratory), the sealed-set discipline, the rule that the classifier AUC is a diagnostic and never a target.
10. **Independent review.** The frozen analysis code is reviewed by a statistics reviewer who did not write it (gate SV-9) before the addendum is committed.
