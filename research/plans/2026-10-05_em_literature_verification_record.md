## 3. What the verified evidence supports, labelled by regime (CLAUDE.md classification)

- **Transferred approximation (generic sampling calorimeters, not AMS-specific, no thin-calorimeter treatment):** the structural form of the Grindhammer-Peters generator: gamma longitudinal profile; (ln T, ln alpha) correlated bivariate Gaussian with rho about 0.7-0.78; sampling shift; deterministic lateral profile in tau plus spot-level fluctuation with the coupling tau_i. Its coefficients were fitted to a different set of calorimeters and are not claimed for this detector.
- **AMS-specific evidence:** geometry; test-beam energy resolution as in Li et al.; dead-cell and side-leakage corrections as in Zhang et al.
- **Unresolved:** fluctuation magnitudes and correlations for THIS detector; rear-leakage fluctuations at 10-100 GeV (the CDF 5% at 50 GeV is for a different detector); the 0.35% / 0.45% leakage statement; thin-calorimeter behaviour.

## 4. What this does and does not change (for the researcher; not a decision)

The verified source describes TWO fluctuating longitudinal variables with a positive correlation and a spot-level lateral fluctuation. The DEC-001 generator, as recorded in `DECISIONS.md`, uses one lognormal depth variable per event with beta fixed at 0.65 and a deterministic lateral profile; the exploration comparison found too little longitudinal spread (RMS 30.9-31.8 mm against 31.4-36.9 mm) and too smooth a lateral profile (too many hit cells). The structural match is a **hypothesis to be tested on Geant4 electrons, not a finding**; the new generator's parameters are to be calibrated to the Geant4 electrons, with the literature supplying forms and constraints, not numbers.

## 4b. Adversarial pass (step 3), 2026-10-05

An adversarial agent tested the proposition "the Grindhammer-Peters structure is an adequate prior for this detector" on the unsealed exploration electrons and searched the literature for counterevidence. **Its headline was wrong and is superseded by a control that I ran.**

- The agent reported a (ln T, ln alpha) correlation of 0.87-0.90 against the literature's 0.57-0.62 and called the literature formula "REFUTED in the thin regime", a "physics finding". I could not reproduce 0.87-0.90 with either of two fit estimators, and the agent gave no control. A planted-correlation control (`ams_ecal.em_longitudinal_fluctuations`, tests in `tests/test_em_longitudinal_fluctuations.py`) shows that the fits do not inflate a correlation: planted 0 is recovered as -0.12 to 0.04, planted 0.6 as 0.44-0.60 (least squares) and 0.28-0.54 (cumulative). The agent's lateral-coupling test failed on an API error and its literature search added nothing beyond this record.
- **Result (`results/em_generator/longitudinal_fluctuations.json`, exploration electrons, E_c = 7.6 MeV from the repository's config):** the data agree with the Grindhammer-Peters sampling formulas to within about 11%. sigma(ln T) data / formula: 0.154 / 0.154 (10 GeV), 0.149 / 0.136 (20), 0.124 / 0.118 (50), 0.119 / 0.107 (100). sigma(ln alpha): 0.209 / 0.206, 0.205 / 0.185, 0.168 / 0.163, 0.160 / 0.150. Correlation (least squares / cumulative fit, formula): 0.62 / 0.69 (0.62), 0.60 / 0.68 (0.60), 0.59 / 0.65 (0.58), 0.51 / 0.56 (0.57). The measured spreads include the fit's own noise, so the intrinsic spreads are somewhat smaller. Median contained fraction 0.951, 0.936, 0.915, 0.890 (10-100 GeV).
- **Lateral coupling, measured, not modelled** (Spearman correlation of an event's lateral observable with its fitted ln T, at 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 GeV): width -0.27 / -0.37 / -0.56 / -0.70; hit cells -0.56 / -0.61 / -0.64 / -0.68; core fraction +0.38 / +0.50 / +0.66 / +0.78; containment +0.40 / +0.49 / +0.69 / +0.83. Events whose maximum is deeper are narrower and more concentrated, and the coupling grows with energy. Not yet controlled: entry-cell phase and leakage as confounders. A generator with a deterministic lateral profile cannot show this.
- **What stays unverified:** the marginal distributions are not bivariate Gaussian according to the agent (ln alpha non-normal, skewness 0.3-0.6, Shapiro p < 1e-6), not independently checked, and possibly partly a fit artefact; whether the estimator choice moves it is open. The agent's search found no published contradiction, but it was brief; absence is not evidence.
- **Reading for the researcher (not a decision):** at the level of the longitudinal fluctuation magnitudes and their correlation, the transferred Grindhammer-Peters forms describe these Geant4 electrons well, which supports using them as the structural prior. The DEC-001 generator, per `src/ams_ecal/longitudinal.py` lines 323-331, samples the shape parameter alpha with beta fixed, so ln T and ln alpha are perfectly correlated there (rho = 1 against about 0.6 measured), and its lateral profile is deterministic, against a measured lateral-longitudinal coupling of up to 0.8. Those two differences are candidate explanations of the exploration comparison's failures, to be tested, not asserted.
