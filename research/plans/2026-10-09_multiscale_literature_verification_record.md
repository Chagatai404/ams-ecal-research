## What the literature gives the test, and what it does not

1. **The premise has support in simulation:** a box-count dimension of hit counts separates electromagnetic from hadronic showers in a highly granular simulated HCAL (Ruan et al.), at q = 0, together with the hit count. It is not shown for AMS, for energy-weighted measures, for q = 1 or 2, or independent of the hit count.
2. **There is a counter-indication:** the only EM-shower multifractal-analysis paper in hand finds D_q about equal (homogeneous). The pre-registration must allow, and the paper must be able to report, a negative result.
3. **The conventional-feature control is mandatory**, because the only positive evidence combines FD with the hit count.
4. **The estimator cautions** (finite-size artefacts, q >= 0, a null that can fail) are supported at abstract level only and are already built into the controls; they stay "transferred, gap" until the papers are read beyond the abstract.
5. **Open gaps that do not block the test but are labelled:** the two primary sources (Hentschel-Procaccia, Bialas-Peschanski), the three q < 0 snippets, and the Pattern Recognition paper.

Verification of the Geant4 side is in section 3 of the plan (one list, one cut, normal incidence) and is addressed by the systematics sets G-syst and the existing proton variants.
