## 3. What this changes

1. **The answer to G3 in part:** gain-type effects (attenuation, equalization, HG/LG ratio, temperature, PMT position) are inverted at cell or layer level **before** clustering; adding them to the simulated readout as distortions would apply an effect AMS removed. What remains are the **stochastic** effects: photoelectron statistics (documented, about 8 p.e./MIP), pedestal noise and the zero-suppression threshold (value missing), cross-talk (value missing), dead-cell handling.
2. **R-A and R-B split stays right.** R-A (fibre energy from deposit, calibrated on Geant4) is unaffected. R-B becomes a short list: Poisson photoelectron smearing (documented), a threshold and noise scan, a cross-talk scan; everything else is "already corrected".
3. **Review item "ECAL energy knowledge" gets a precise source:** the cell energies that AMS uses are the corrected ones (E1), so a simulation of the **corrected** cell energy is the right target.
4. **The weak spot narrows:** with NIM A 714 and the two equalization papers read, the **ordering is documented in two independent AMS papers** (E1 and E6: attenuation, then equalization, then HG/LG, temperature offline), the **residual gain error is documented** (2.4%, 1.5%) and the number of low-response cells is known (8). Still **not found** in any source in hand: the readout zero-suppression threshold, a cross-talk value, the AMS Birks constant, the saturation level and a residual attenuation error.
5. **A discrepancy to carry, not smooth over:** the PMT temperature slope is 0.25 %/degC in the flight talk (E1) and about 1e-3 per degC in the instrument paper (E6); both say it is corrected offline, so neither enters the simulation, but no single number should be quoted.
