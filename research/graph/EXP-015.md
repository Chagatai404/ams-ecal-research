# EXP-015 — realistic-regime Geant4 pilot and its matched analysis

Approved by the researcher in the session chat on 2026-11-09 ("I approve it from here"). Electrons 300 per band (50-2000 GeV), protons 1000 per band (50-4000 GeV), cos-weighted incidence up to 25 degrees, full face, E^-1 generation in bands with physical spectra as weights. The pre-stated per-band matching was not testable (2-40 pairs). The post-hoc pooled matching (234 pairs; matched protons carry a median 4.4x the electron energy) shows D1 and D2 differ (KS 0.41-0.50, Holm p 6e-4) while D0 does not, and the difference survives photoelectron smearing. EXPLORATORY, post hoc. Details: `research/COMPLETED_WORK.md` section 4.7.
