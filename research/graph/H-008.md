# H-008 — H-A: electromagnetic showers are nearly monofractal after conditioning (PROPOSED)

After conditioning on energy and occupancy, the finite-scale statistics (D0, D1, D2) of electromagnetic showers depend on energy only through quantities the conditioning already carries. "No residual energy trend beyond the surrogate bands" CONFIRMS this hypothesis. Prior (agent's reading of V3, Anchordoqui et al.): D_q about equal and about 1 in the scaling region. Falsified by a residual energy trend in D2 beyond the cell-shuffle and Poisson surrogates. Descriptive curves only, no confirmatory primary.
