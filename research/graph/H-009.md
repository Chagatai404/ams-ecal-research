# H-009 — H-B: hadronic heterogeneity beyond conventional morphology (PROPOSED; endpoint P1)

Interacting hadronic showers show finite-scale heterogeneity beyond what occupancy, total energy, depth and width explain. Named primary P1 (stratum-weighted KS of D2, eligible in both classes, matched on occupancy and energy, permutation within strata). Prior: weak; the closest precedent (V2, HEGRA) found multifractal parameters strongly correlated with conventional shape variables and only a slight combined gain, and the only calorimeter FD paper (V1) shows FD tied to log N_hits. A null result is plausible and publishable. Falsified if P1 lies within the strata-matched null or its sign changes under a physics-list variant. Status proposed.
