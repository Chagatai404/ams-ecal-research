# EXP-017 — effective fibre saturation derived from the published AMS hottest-cell curve

Approved by the researcher on 2026-11-09 ("Approve EXP-017"). Our Geant4 hottest-cell energy without saturation matches the AMS simulation without saturation within 3%. A one-parameter per-fibre law e' = e/(1 + e/e_s) gives e_s = 260 (+107/-68) MeV; it over-suppresses by 4-6% at 200-400 GeV (AMS saturation has a later onset). A project phenomenological calibration from a published figure, not the AMS parametrisation; off by default. Details: `research/COMPLETED_WORK.md` section 4.7.
