# H-002 — Physical multiscale difference (H1 of the RQ-001 note)

Detailed electromagnetic and proton-induced showers exhibit measurably different scale-dependent spatial statistics over part of the accessible scale range.

**Update 2026-10-10:** DEVELOPMENT evidence only. Electrons and interacting protons differ in D0, D1, D2 and D0 - D2 (matched on occupied cells, KS 0.3-0.55, split-half floor 0.02-0.10, deposition grid, 10-100 GeV, exposed Geant4). This is not the registered test and may be redundant with width and energy (`research/COMPLETED_WORK.md`, C1). The only EM-shower multifractal paper read in full text (Anchordoqui et al. 2000) found generalised dimensions roughly equal, a counter-indication.
