# H-004 — Incremental information (H3 of the RQ-001 note)

Validated multiscale descriptors add particle-ID information beyond conventional observables (shower maximum, widths, containment, leakage, energy, sparsity).

**Update 2026-10-10:** DEVELOPMENT evidence only. In ideal Geant4 events the conventional features already separate electrons from interacting protons (AUC 1.0000, also without total energy and occupied cells) and the multiscale features add +-0.001, so the registered added-information rule cannot be met in that regime; this does not weaken or support H-004. The regime question is open (`research/RESEARCH_PLAN.md`, C5, DECIDE-1).
