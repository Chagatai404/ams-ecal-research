# H-004 — Incremental information (H3 of the RQ-001 note)

Validated multiscale descriptors add particle-ID information beyond conventional observables (shower maximum, widths, containment, leakage, energy, sparsity).

**Update 2026-10-10:** DEVELOPMENT evidence only. In ideal Geant4 events the conventional features already separate electrons from interacting protons (AUC 1.0000, also without total energy and occupied cells) and the multiscale features add +-0.001, so the registered added-information rule cannot be met in that regime; this does not weaken or support H-004. The regime question is open (`research/COMPLETED_WORK.md`, C5, DECIDE-1).

**Update 2026-11-11:** EXPLORATORY evidence in the AMS-like realistic regime (simulation only). On a hard subset matched on occupied cells and readout energy, adding readout D0/D1/D2 to 24 conventional variables gave no gain as large as 0.010 in class-balanced log-loss (EXP-019: +0.0012, 95% interval -0.0048 to +0.0072, 448 electrons and 640 protons; EXP-018 at pilot size agreed). This bounds the incremental information at the scintillator level in this subset; under simulated photoelectron smearing and fibre saturation the answer is inconclusive. H-004 is neither refuted nor supported in general (`research/COMPLETED_WORK.md` section 4.7).
